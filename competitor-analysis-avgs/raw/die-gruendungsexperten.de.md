# Competitive Intelligence Report: die-gruendungsexperten.de

**Research conducted:** 2026-08-13 | **Method:** Live WebFetch/curl of homepage, AVGS landing page, Gründungszuschuss/Gründerzuschuss pages, blog, Impressum, team page, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, plus WebSearch corroboration (ProvenExpert, LinkedIn, Northdata, local press).

---

## 1. Profile

- **[Data]** Brand of Beratungsdienst Paulisch GmbH (Löcknitzer Straße 10, 17309 Pasewalk, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern). AG Neubrandenburg, HRB 7506. MD: Silke Paulisch. A related entity Die Gründungsexperten GmbH (HRB 21330, founded 2021-03-16, €25,000 capital, MDs Jannes & Thomas Paulisch) exists at the same address — relationship between entities not fully disclosed [data gap].
- **[Data]** Offerings: AVGS-funded Existenzgründercoaching, Gründerzuschuss application support, "Führerschein für Unternehmer" paid course, entrepreneur-type/business-idea self-tests, webinars, "Gründercoach werden" recruitment funnel. Also runs an unrelated "Alltagsbegleitung" (care services) line on the same site — notable scope creep.
- **[Data]** Target: unemployed, Bürgergeld recipients, career returners, Rehabilitanden (named explicitly on AVGS page).
- **[Data]** LinkedIn: 2-10 direct employees; 37-45 freelance coaches nationwide (sources differ slightly — treat as approximate).
- **[Data]** Brand claims heritage "since 1996"; GmbH entity founded 2011 — brand story vs. legal founding date, not contradictory.
- **[Data]** Claims "2,184+ successful business foundings." ProvenExpert: **362 aggregated reviews, 4.89/5, 99% recommend** (177 native + 185 from Google/Facebook). One source cites "~3,000 clients over 13 years" — inconsistent with 2,184 figure, likely different methodologies.

- **[Data]** 4 named on-site testimonials with photos/quotes, reinforced by 362-review ProvenExpert aggregate — genuinely strong verifiable trust signal.
- **[Data]** Named leadership (Silke, Jannes, Thomas Paulisch) + back-office/sales staff with role descriptions on dedicated team page. No individual coach qualifications/certifications itemized.
- **[Data]** No structured, in-depth case studies — only short testimonial quotes, no outcome narratives.

## 3. SEO & On-Page Structure (live-fetched 2026-08-13)

**Homepage**
- Title: "Selbstständig machen mit Förderung | Ideen zum Selbstständig machen"
- Meta description present, benefit-oriented.
- 3× `<h1>` tags found (likely page-builder duplication artifact, not 3 visually distinct H1s — still a technical dup flag).
- ~1,300-1,500 words.
- **Zero `application/ld+json` blocks found** — no Organization, LocalBusiness, FAQPage, or Review schema anywhere checked.
- Platform: Duda website builder (confirmed via asset host fingerprints and generic Duda-template robots.txt).

**AVGS landing page** (`/avgs-existenzgruendercoaching`)
- Title: "AVGS Existenzgründercoaching – Kostenlose Gründungsberatung mit Gutschein"
- Meta description present and benefit-oriented.
- 2× H1 (duplicate, builder artifact), 17× H2, 24× H3, 14× H4 — deep, logically organized (Fördermöglichkeiten, Antragsberechtigte, process, FAQ).
- ~1,500 words. Canonical tag correct. OG tags complete. **JSON-LD: none.**
- Internal linking: ~19 unique targets, nearly all repeated nav/footer links — no contextual in-body links to Businessplan/Finanzierung content (because none exists).

**Keyword cannibalization risk**: Two separate landing pages for near-synonymous topics — `/gruendungszuschuss-arbeitsamt` and `/gruenderzuschuss` — likely compete against each other in search.

**robots.txt / sitemap.xml**: Generic unmodified Duda-template robots.txt (disallows `/Login`, `/store/checkout` etc. — irrelevant to actual content). Sitemap: 57 URLs total, flat, single. Only **one** blog post URL indexed; no Businessplan/Finanzierung/Fördermittel hub pages.

## 4. Content Strategy

- Blog hub is a **JS-rendered widget** with essentially no static/crawlable archive — sitemap confirms only **one indexed post** as of 2026-08-13 ("Selbstständig statt arbeitslos...", published 2025-11-04, ~2,800 words, cites KfW-Gründungsmonitor 2024).
- Cadence: effectively **near-dormant**, no evidence of regular publishing.
- Topic coverage: Gründungszuschuss well covered (2 landing pages + 1 post); AVGS/Fördermittel strong with FAQ; **no Businessplan content**; **no Finanzierung/KfW content** beyond passing testimonial mentions. Clear content gap vs. competitors covering these adjacent topics.
- Per-page depth is good where content exists (1,300-1,500 words, FAQ-structured) — the problem is breadth, not quality.

## 5. Strengths

1. Strong verifiable social proof: 362 reviews at 4.89/5 (99% recommend) via ProvenExpert.
2. Legitimate, disclosed AZAV + ISO 9001 certification, prominently displayed.
3. Deep single-topic landing pages: thorough FAQ, clear step-by-step process, good heading structure.
4. Scaled coach network: 37-45 freelance coaches nationwide — meaningful delivery-capacity advantage.
5. Named leadership with visible continuity (Paulisch family) — supports E-E-A-T/authenticity.
6. Diversified funnel: free eligibility-check tools, entrepreneur-type test, business-idea test as lead magnets.

## 6. Weaknesses

1. Blog effectively inactive/undiscoverable: one indexed post total via a poorly-crawlable JS widget — significant, verifiable content-marketing gap.
2. Zero structured data anywhere checked — no FAQPage/Review schema despite having FAQ content and hundreds of real reviews that would benefit from it.
3. No published AZAV Zulassungsnummer — missed trust/verifiability opportunity.
4. Topic breadth gap: no Businessplan or Finanzierung/KfW content at all.
5. Likely keyword cannibalization between two Gründungszuschuss/Gründerzuschuss pages.
6. Generic, uncustomized technical SEO layer (irrelevant Duda-boilerplate robots.txt, duplicate H1s from templating).
7. Corporate structure ambiguity: two GmbHs at the same address, relationship undisclosed.
8. Inconsistent traction numbers across sources (2,184 vs. ~3,000 clients).

## 7. Data Gaps

- Exact AZAV Zulassungsnummer / issuing fachkundige Stelle not found anywhere public.
- Relationship between Beratungsdienst Paulisch GmbH and Die Gründungsexperten GmbH unclear.
- Revenue, profit, paid-ad spend not disclosed.
- Whether the JS blog widget holds additional unindexed posts not surfaced in sitemap — not verifiable without a JS-rendering crawl.
- Individual coach-level certifications not itemized.
- Historical traffic/ranking trend data would require a paid SEO tool (not run in this pass).
- Paid search/Google Ads presence not checked.
- Social follower counts/cadence not surfaced via WebSearch.
