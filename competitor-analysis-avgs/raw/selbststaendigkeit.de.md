## 5. Strengths

1. Content scale and topical breadth: ~1,450 posts + 524 pages covering the full Gründer lifecycle, not just AVGS — far more long-tail keyword surface than a narrower competitor.
2. Local SEO programmatic footprint: 46 city-specific AVGS landing pages — deliberate systematic local-intent capture.
3. Marketplace/network model (60+ coaches) scales delivery capacity beyond a single-consultancy Träger.
4. Genuine diversified review base: 231 reviews across Google/Facebook/Kundennote (4.95 avg) — real, checkable, multi-platform.
5. Established brand (founded 2014, ~12 years), paid Handelsblatt advertorial signals real marketing spend.
6. Strong on-page SEO fundamentals: clean single-H1 hierarchy, clean URLs, working sitemaps, Ahrefs access allowed.

## 6. Weaknesses

1. No visible AZAV Träger-Zulassungsnummer anywhere on the public site — claimed in prose only.
2. Certification badge naming inconsistency ("Certuria" vs "Centuria") — undermines credibility precision.
3. Case studies in "Erfolgsgeschichten" are generic/unverifiable: no names, photos, video, or hard metrics.
4. Only one named team bio (Roul Radeke); the 60+ coach network isn't individually credentialed — prospects can't vet their actual assigned coach pre-conversion.
5. Duplicate/conflicting JSON-LD schema (Yoast + Rank Math + unlinked AggregateRating) — technical hygiene issue, borderline against Google's review-schema guidance.
6. Marketplace model cuts both ways: outsourced delivery may mean less consistency vs. an in-house boutique team — a differentiation angle nxtmilestone could credibly claim (more personal, hands-on).
