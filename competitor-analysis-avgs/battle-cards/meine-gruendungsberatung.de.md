*Skill: startup-competitors | Generated: 2026-08-13*

## Who they are
Brand of Limburg Consulting (Düsseldorf, parent firm founded 2003), a 7-consultant team. 382 reviews at 4.93/5 via ProvenExpert. Markets as nationwide but is regionally concentrated in NRW/Rhineland-Ruhr.

## Their strengths
- Deep, credentialed team page (7-8 named consultants with real professional bios).
- Genuinely strong independent social proof (382 ProvenExpert reviews, 4.93/5), actively linked from the homepage.
- Backed by an established 20+ year consulting firm, not a lead-gen shell site.
- Large content library (93 posts) with good breadth across Fördermittel/Businessplan/Finanzierung/Gründungszuschuss.
- Interactive eligibility quiz on conversion-critical pages.

## Their weaknesses
- **The word "AZAV" appears nowhere as indexable text on any page checked** — the certificate exists only as an unreadable image. This is both an SEO gap and a direct GEO/AI-citability gap: an AI answer engine cannot cite a credential that only exists as a JPEG.
- Primary AVGS landing page has **zero semantic H2/H3 tags** — heavy visual styling masking a real on-page SEO deficiency.
- Stale, generic, copy-pasted `ProfessionalService` schema across pages with a stale aggregateRating (5.0/75) contradicting their live 382-review ProvenExpert figure.
- Only one embedded testimonial in page copy despite hundreds of reviews existing elsewhere.
- No case-studies/success-stories page anywhere in their 41-page sitemap.
- "Deutschlandweit" positioning is only loosely supported by an actually NRW/Ruhr-concentrated city-page footprint.

## How to win against them
Their AZAV-as-image problem is their single most exploitable weakness and maps directly onto nxtmilestone's own GEO/AI-citability interest: publishing AZAV certification as real, structured, indexable text/schema would leapfrog them specifically on machine-readability, a dimension they've completely missed despite otherwise-solid content.

## When they win over you
