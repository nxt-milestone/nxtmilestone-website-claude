# Battle Card: selbststaendigkeit.de

*Skill: startup-competitors | Generated: 2026-08-13*

## Who they are
Radeke Management GmbH's founder portal brand (est. 2014, Duisburg). Runs AVGS Gründercoaching as a **60+ coach marketplace**, not a boutique consultancy, wrapped in a huge content operation (~1,450 blog posts, ~524 pages, 46 city landing pages). 231 aggregated reviews at 4.95/5 across Google/Facebook/Kundennote.

## Their strengths
- Unmatched content scale and topical breadth (AVGS + Businessplan + Fördermittel + Finanzierung + Recht + Steuern, all covered in depth).
- Solid local SEO footprint: 46 flat city-level landing pages nationwide (no sub-district granularity — that's a firmenkompass.de tactic, not this one).
- Genuine, diversified, multi-platform review base.
- Active same-day publishing confirmed — a live, not stale, operation.

## Their weaknesses
- No published AZAV Zulassungsnummer despite certification claims.
- Duplicate/conflicting JSON-LD from two competing SEO plugins (Yoast + Rank Math), plus an unlinked AggregateRating schema block.
- Case studies are generic and unverifiable (no names, photos, or numbers).
- Only one named team bio (the founder); the 60+ coach network isn't individually credentialed — prospects can't vet their actual assigned coach.
- Marketplace/outsourced-delivery model may mean less consistency than an in-house team.

## How to win against them
Don't compete on content volume — you will lose that fight. Compete on: (1) a real, checkable AZAV number they don't have, (2) clean, correct structured data where theirs is duplicated/broken, (3) named, credentialed, in-house delivery (if that's true of nxtmilestone) versus their directory-matched network, (4) genuinely quantified case studies where theirs are generic placeholders.

## When they win over you
If a prospect's decision criterion is pure information depth/topic coverage (e.g. they want to research every founding topic in one place before ever talking to a human), selbststaendigkeit.de's sheer content scale wins by default.

## Customer objections & responses
- *"They have way more articles/guides than you."* → "Volume isn't the same as a real person who reviews your specific case — and their coaching is delivered by whichever of 60+ independent coaches you're matched with, not a dedicated in-house team."
- *"They show more reviews."* → Match with your own real, verifiable review presence rather than disputing their number.

## Key vulnerability to exploit
Their AZAV claim is unverifiable (no number published) and their structured data is internally inconsistent — both are concrete, checkable facts, not opinion.

## Churn signals (why their customers might leave)
Directory/marketplace model — inconsistent coach quality or fit is a plausible churn driver, though not directly evidenced in this research (no forum/review-complaint mining was performed for this pass).

## Red Flags
🚩 Duplicate/conflicting schema markup could confuse rich-result eligibility — a real technical SEO defect, not just a stylistic nitpick.

## Yellow Flags
🟡 "Certuria"/"Centuria" certification badge naming is inconsistent across their own pages — unclear which is correct.
