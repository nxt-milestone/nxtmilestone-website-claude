# Battle Card: die-gruendungsexperten.de

*Skill: startup-competitors | Generated: 2026-08-13*

## Who they are
Brand of Beratungsdienst Paulisch GmbH (Pasewalk, family-run, brand story since 1996, GmbH founded 2011). 37-45 freelance coach network. 362 reviews at 4.89/5 via ProvenExpert.

## Their strengths
- Strong, verifiable social proof (362 reviews, 99% recommend).
- Legitimate, disclosed AZAV + ISO 9001:2015 certification, prominently shown.
- Deep, thorough FAQ-structured AVGS landing page.
- Scaled coach network (37-45 freelancers nationwide).
- Named, continuous family leadership since the 1990s/2011.

## Their weaknesses
- Blog is effectively dead: **one** indexed post, loaded via a barely-crawlable JS widget.
- **Zero structured data anywhere** — no Organization/FAQPage/Review schema despite having FAQ content and hundreds of reviews that would directly benefit.
- No published AZAV Zulassungsnummer.
- Zero Businessplan or Finanzierung/KfW-loan content — entirely uncontested adjacent-topic territory.
- Two similarly-named landing pages (Gründungszuschuss vs. Gründerzuschuss) likely cannibalizing each other in search.
- An unrelated "Alltagsbegleitung" (care services) offering on the same site is confusing scope creep.
- Runs two GmbHs at the same address with an undisclosed relationship between them.

## How to win against them
This is the most out-publishable competitor in the set on pure content: their blog is dead and they have zero coverage of Businessplan/Finanzierung. A modest, sustained nxtmilestone content cadence covering exactly those gaps would win outright on freshness and breadth.

## When they win over you
Their FAQ-depth on the core AVGS page and long operating history (since 1996/2011) give an impression of stability a newer or thinner page can't match at a glance.

## Customer objections & responses
- *"They've been around since the 90s."* → Longevity is real, but their content hasn't been touched in years — pair your own credibility signals with visibly current, well-maintained content.

## Key vulnerability to exploit
Total content-marketing dormancy plus a total absence of structured data — both concrete, checkable, and directly actionable as a contrast point.

## Churn signals
Not directly evidenced in this research pass.

## Yellow Flags
