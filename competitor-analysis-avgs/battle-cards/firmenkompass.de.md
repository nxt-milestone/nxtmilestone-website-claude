# Battle Card: firmenkompass.de

*Skill: startup-competitors | Generated: 2026-08-13*

## Who they are
Firmenkompass GmbH, Frankfurt, founded 2023 — the youngest competitor in the set, but the most aggressive programmatic-SEO operation: 143 of its 181 indexed pages (~79%) are city/district-specific AVGS landing pages.

## Their strengths
- The **only** competitor with a specific, checkable AZAV registration (Träger-Nr. T-04665-3218, Maßnahme-Nr., named certifying body TQCert, expiry date) — the strongest trust-transparency signal in the entire set.
- Sophisticated programmatic local SEO: deliberate sub-district pages only in Berlin/Hamburg, framed as "online coaching for founders in [city]" (avoids fake-local-office NAP risk).
- Multilingual delivery (DE/EN/TR/RU/UA) — a real edge for migrant/foreign-national founders.
- Dedicated B2G page targeting Arbeitsagenturen/Jobcenter directly — a referral channel outside pure SEO.
- Homepage has properly implemented Organization + FAQPage + WebSite schema.

## Their weaknesses
- **Confirmed, concrete QA failure**: the Berlin-Marzahn page's body substantially describes the wrong district (Berlin-Pankow), including which Jobcenter/Agentur für Arbeit is responsible — plus a typo in its own H1. Hard evidence of an unreviewed template/AI-generation pipeline.
- Thin, near-verbatim templated meta descriptions/H2 structure across dozens of pages — classic doorway-page pattern.
- **No blog or editorial content of any kind** — zero non-location, non-product content.
- Very small social footprint (78 LinkedIn followers) versus a claimed 1,400+ clients.
- "10+ Jahre Erfahrung" claim sits awkwardly next to the company's own 2023 founding date.
- Inconsistent schema across its 143 location pages (FAQ schema on only some).

## How to win against them
Use their own confirmed content-quality bug as the cautionary tale for why nxtmilestone's content (if it pursues any city/location pages later) must be genuinely reviewed, not templated blind. On trust: match their AZAV-number transparency exactly — this single move would tie nxtmilestone with the strongest player in the market on this specific dimension.

## When they win over you
Anyone who finds one of their 143 city-page results directly via a hyper-local search query ("AVGS Gründungscoaching Berlin Marzahn") will land on a page built exactly for that query — nxtmilestone currently has no equivalent local-intent pages at all.

## Customer objections & responses
- *"They show up for my exact city."* → Acknowledge the real gap and consider a smaller, carefully-QA'd set of location pages once trust/schema fundamentals are fixed (see action plan) — not before.

## Key vulnerability to exploit
The Marzahn/Pankow content-mismatch bug is a hard, verifiable fact — a specific, real quality failure in their flagship SEO strategy.

## Churn signals
Not directly evidenced in this research pass.

🚩 Homepage displays government-agency logos (BAFA, BMAS, KfW, Bundesagentur für Arbeit) alongside its own brand in a "trusted partners" row — could visually overstate an official partnership; worth independent verification before treating as a template to copy.
